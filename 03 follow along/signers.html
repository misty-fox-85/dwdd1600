Who were the signers

Famous signers

Some of the most famous signers of the Declaration of Independence include.

John Hancock
President of the Continental Congress and famous for his large, bold signature on the Declaration of Independence.
Benjamin Franklin
A Founding Father, inventor, scientist, diplomat, and one of the most well-known signers of the Declaration of Independence.
Thomas Jefferson
The main author of the Declaration of Independence and later the third President of the United States.
John Adams
A strong supporter of American independence who later became the second President of the United States.
Samuel Adams
A political leader from Massachusetts who strongly supported independence from Great Britain.
Benjamin Harrison
A Virginia politician and signer of the Declaration of Independence who later served as governor of Virginia.

Each of these individuals played a significant role in the founding of the United States and is remembered for their courage in signing the Declaration, an act considered treasonous by the British Crown.
