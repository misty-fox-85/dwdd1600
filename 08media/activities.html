Types of Meditation Activities

Activity Main Focus Purpose
Mindfulness Present Moment Build Awareness
Breathing Your Breath Reduce Stress
Guided Following A Guide Relaxation
Body Scan Body Sensations Release Tension
Loving-Kindness Compassion Encourage Kindness
Zen Sitting in Stillness Develope Inner Peace

Mindfulness

"Focus on the present moment and observe your thoughts and feelings without judgment."

Breathing

"Focus on your breath and let go of distracting thoughts."

Guided

"Follow along with a guide or instructor to lead you through the meditation practice."

Body Scan

"Systematically focus on different parts of your body to promote relaxation and awareness."

Loving-Kindness

"Focus on developing feelings of compassion and love towards yourself and others."

Transcendental

"Use a mantra or repeated word to help focus your mind and achieve a state of deep relaxation."

Zen

"Focus on sitting in stillness and observing your thoughts and sensations without attachment."
