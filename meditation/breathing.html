Simple Breathing Exercise

  1. Find a quiet place to sit or lie down.
  2. Close your eyes and take a deep breath in through your nose.
  3. Hold the breath for a few seconds.
  4. Exhale slowly through your mouth.
  5. Repeat this process for several minutes.

Box Breathing

Box breathing is a technique that involves inhaling, holding, exhaling, and holding the breath again, each for an equal count.

  1. Inhale for 4 seconds.
  2. Hold for 4 seconds.
  3. Exhale for 4 seconds.
  4. Hold for 4 seconds.
  5. Repeat the cycle for several minutes.
