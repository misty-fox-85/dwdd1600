Digital citizenship

what is digital citizenship?

digital citizenship is the responsible, safe, and ethical use of digital technologies, including the internet, social media, computers, and mobile devices. It means using technology in ways that respect yourself, others, and the law.

digital law

Digital law refers to the laws and rules that govern how people use technology, computers, the internet, and digital information.

what is AUP?

An Acceptable Use Policy is a set of rules that explains how people are allowed to use a school's, company's, or organization's computers, networks, internet, and other technology.

what is plagiarism

Plagiarism is using someone else's words, ideas, images, or work and presenting them as your own without giving proper credit.
